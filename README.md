# Cathy Liu

I am a biotechnology graduate with a background in wet-lab research and computational biology. I completed my Master of Biotechnology at the University of Melbourne.

I want to use AI and quantitative models to answer biological questions: how cells and neural systems change state, how we can measure those changes, and how they respond to perturbations. Longer term, I hope to pursue a PhD and work toward intelligent, minimally invasive ways to sense and modulate the nervous system.

## Selected projects

**[Robust optimization in virtual epilepsy models](https://github.com/liuzhitong330/tvb-llm-robust-neurostim)**  
Can an LLM-guided search find model parameters that remain useful across simulated variation? Python implementation, stored experiments, tests, and a research essay. In-silico results—not clinical efficacy.

**[Seizure-forecast operating points](https://github.com/liuzhitong330/neural-state-forecast-operating-points)**  
How does forecast sensitivity change across participants at the same false-positive-rate budget? An interactive reanalysis of published ROC data, with explicit limits on clinical interpretation. [Open analysis](https://liuzhitong330.github.io/neural-state-forecast-operating-points/).

**[Donor-aware single-cell marker review](https://github.com/liuzhitong330/franklin-huang-lab-demo)**  
Which epithelial marker contrasts persist across donors rather than being driven by donors contributing more cells? Processed-count analysis, sensitivity checks, source provenance, and reproducibility instructions. [Open analysis](https://liuzhitong330.github.io/franklin-huang-lab-demo/).
